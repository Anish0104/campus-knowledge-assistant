eduroam

eduroam is a free and secure wireless network available to the Rutgers community when visiting participating universities.

Why use eduroam?

Connect to free WiFi at member universities in more than 100 countries

Install on your device and log in with your NetID

Security exceeds typical commercial hotspots

Visitors from member institutions can connect at Rutgers

Instructions for installing and connecting to eduroam

Rutgers students, staff, and faculty

Configure your device and log in with your NetID and password.

Visitors from participating institutions

Work with your home institution to get set up for eduroam.

Help & Support

Get help

Members of the Rutgers community can contact the Help Desk if they run into issues connecting to eduroam. ( NOTE : Visitors should contact IT staff at their home institution.)

Looking for RUWireless?

Members of the Rutgers community can access a wireless connection through RUWireless Secure while guests can receive access through RUWireless.
